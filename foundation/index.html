1 · Brand and theme

Light and dark are first-class themes. Every semantic role keeps its name; only the resolved value changes. The logo is not present in this repository and is not recreated here — the original keeps its non-rounded form.

Text hierarchy

Primary text — the default voice for every page.

Secondary text — supporting copy that stays readable.

Tertiary text — hints and metadata; still at least 4.5:1.

Disabled text — contrast-exempt but perceivable.

Inverse text on an inverted surface.

Overlay

Page content behind the scrim. The scrim signals modality; it never claims separation in dark mode.

Dialog surface

2 · Neutral palette

Thirteen frozen steps, shared by every site. Pure neutral, C = 0.

3 · Typography

Inter for Latin, Noto Sans SC for CJK, self-hosted. Two weights only: 400 and 600. Zero tracking on every role.

4 · Spacing

A 4px grid with a 2px half-step. No free values.
